About GetXH

We're the team that makes sure no lead slips.

A small, focused automation studio based in India. We build WhatsApp AI assistants for real estate and service businesses — so every enquiry gets answered in seconds, qualified, and booked, even after hours.

Why we started

Born from one frustrating problem: speed

We kept watching real estate businesses lose good leads — not because their properties were bad, but because nobody replied after business hours. A buyer would message at 10 PM, get no answer, and book a visit with whoever responded first the next morning.

So we built GetXH to solve that one thing first: never let a lead wait. An AI assistant that replies in seconds, any hour, qualifies the serious buyers, and books the visit — so the deal stays with you, not the faster competitor.

We stayed small and focused on purpose. No bloated software, no jargon, no months-long projects — just practical automation that pays for itself in booked appointments. We started with real estate, and we're growing into clinics, coaching and service businesses.
