FAQ

Service questions

Can I start with just one service?

Absolutely. Most clients start with a Pilot — one workflow, fixed price, 30 days — then add more as they see results.

Will it integrate with my current tools?

Yes — that is the core of what we do. We connect WhatsApp, email, forms and spreadsheets to your CRM, calendar and other tools. Start with a simple spreadsheet upload — no CRM integration needed.

Do you maintain it after setup?

Yes. We monitor, fix and improve your workflows over time so they keep working. You do not have to touch a thing.

How long does setup take?

It depends on the tools and workflows involved. We confirm a timeline on your call.

Does it work with my WhatsApp number?

Yes. We set it up on your own WhatsApp Business number through the official WhatsApp Business API, so customers keep the number they already know.

Can I customise replies?

Yes. Replies are tailored to your business, services and FAQs. The assistant always clearly identifies itself as an AI assistant.

Can I cancel?

After the pilot, month-to-month. Cancel with 30 days' notice.

Do you provide support?

Yes. We provide ongoing support, updates and maintenance to keep your workflows running smoothly.

Is my data secure?

Your data is stored on trusted cloud infrastructure and is never sold or shared. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Does it work internationally?

Yes. We work with businesses in India and the UK, and the system works wherever your customers use WhatsApp.

Can the assistant hand over to a person?

Yes — instantly. The assistant clearly identifies itself as an AI assistant, and a person can take over at any time with the full conversation history.
