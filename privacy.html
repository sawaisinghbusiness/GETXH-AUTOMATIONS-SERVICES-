Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

GetXH Automation ("GetXH", "we", "us") helps businesses automate customer communication and lead handling. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how we protect it. By using our website or services you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Details you give us — name, phone/WhatsApp number, email and business details when you contact us or fill a form.
  • Lead data we process for clients — when we run automation for a client, we process the messages and contact details of that client's leads on their behalf.
  • Basic usage data — standard, non-identifying analytics about how the website is used.

2. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide our services.
  • To set up, run and improve the automation we build for clients.
  • To communicate with you about your project.

3. WhatsApp & third-party platforms

Our automation runs on the official WhatsApp Business API and other reputable cloud providers. Your and your leads' data is handled in line with those platforms' terms and applicable data-protection rules.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We only share it with trusted service providers needed to deliver our service (e.g. messaging and hosting infrastructure), or where required by law.

5. Data security & retention

We use secure cloud infrastructure and reasonable safeguards to protect data, and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. Email sales@getxh.in and we'll action it.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at sales@getxh.in.
