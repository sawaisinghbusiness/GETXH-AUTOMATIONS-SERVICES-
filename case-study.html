The Client

High stakes, fast-moving leads

Sarvottam is a real estate firm where every incoming enquiry represents a potential multi-lakh commission. In this business, leads don't wait — a buyer or investor who messages today expects an answer today, and the agent who responds first is the one who gets the site visit, the negotiation, and ultimately the deal.

Their entire revenue depends on how fast they can turn a cold enquiry into a warm conversation. When someone is in the market to invest in property, they reach out to several agencies at once. The window of opportunity isn't days — it's minutes.

The Problem

₹5 Lakh Walking Out The Door Every Month

Enquiries arrived around the clock — late evenings, weekends, early mornings — but the team could only reply during working hours, and only when they weren't on site visits or calls.

01

The 9 PM Lead Trap

A prospect who messaged at 9 PM got a response the next morning… by which time they had already moved on to another agent who replied faster.

02

Losing the Response Race

In real estate, the first agent to reply wins — and Sarvottam was losing that race. Slow first responses were costing nearly ₹5 lakh every month in lost opportunities.

03

A Repeating Daily Tax

This wasn't a one-time issue. It was a daily, repeating tax on their revenue — baked into how the business operated, invisible until measured.

The Reality Before Automation

“We'd wake up to a phone full of enquiries that had already gone cold. By the time we replied, the buyer had already booked a viewing with someone else.”

The Solution

A Personal AI Assistant, Trained On Their Own Data

We didn't hand them a generic chatbot. We built a personal AI assistant trained specifically on Sarvottam's own listings, pricing, and process — designed to do what their best agent would do in the first two minutes.

1. Replies within seconds — 24/7

Operates around the clock with zero breaks and no downtime. Whether the lead comes in at 2 PM or 2 AM, the response is instant and helpful.

2. Answers professionally & on-brand

Uses the firm's actual property information, pricing guidelines, and tone, so conversations feel personal and authentic, not robotic.

3. Qualifies the lead automatically

Asks the right questions — budget, preferred location, timeline, and purchase intent — so the team knows exactly who they're talking to beforehand.

4. Hands genuine leads to a human

The moment a lead is confirmed serious, the assistant transfers the conversation to a real agent with full context ready. No tyre-kickers, no lost deals.

The Key Difference

The team now only ever talks to serious, ready buyers. Everything before that — the greeting, the FAQ, the qualification — is handled instantly, automatically, around the clock.
