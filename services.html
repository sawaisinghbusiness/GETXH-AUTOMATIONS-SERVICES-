FAQ

Service questions

Can I start with just WhatsApp automation?

Absolutely. Many clients start with the Starter setup to get instant replies live, then add qualification and booking as they see results.

Will it integrate with my current tools?

Yes — we connect to your calendar, and on the Custom package, your CRM. Tell us what you use and we'll handle the integration.

Do you maintain it after setup?

Yes. We monitor, tweak and improve your flows over time so they keep converting. You don't touch a thing.

How fast can I go live?

Starter setups can be live in days. Larger custom builds take a little longer, but we'll give you a clear timeline up front.

Does it work with my WhatsApp number?

Yes. We can set up the AI on your existing WhatsApp Business number, so you don't need to change the number your customers already know.

How long does setup take?

Most setups are completed within 3 to 7 business days, depending on your requirements and integrations.

Can I customize replies?

Yes. Every reply is tailored to your business, services, pricing, FAQs and brand tone, so the AI sounds like your team.

Can I stop anytime?

Yes. There are no long-term contracts. You can pause or cancel your service whenever you choose.

Do you provide support?

Yes. We provide ongoing support, updates and maintenance to keep your AI assistant running smoothly.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Your data is stored securely using trusted cloud infrastructure. We never sell or share your customer data.

Does it work internationally?

Yes. Our AI assistants work for businesses worldwide. As long as your customers use WhatsApp, the system works regardless of location.

Can AI transfer chats to humans?

Yes. When needed, the AI can instantly hand the conversation over to you or your team with the full chat history, so nothing gets lost.
